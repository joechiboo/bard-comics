— 第 1 頁 —

雷歐提斯

行李都上船了，妹妹。至於哈姆雷特殿下那些殷勤——當它是年輕人的一時興致吧。像一朵早春的紫羅蘭，開得早，謝得也快。

My necessaries are embark'd... For Hamlet and the trifling of his favour, hold it a fashion and a toy in blood: a violet in the youth of primy nature, forward, not permanent.

奧菲莉亞

不過如此嗎？好哥哥，我記住了。可是你自己別學那些壞牧師——指給人家上天堂的荊棘路，自己倒在花街柳巷裡流連。

No more but so?... But, good my brother, do not, as some ungracious pastors do, show me the steep and thorny way to heaven; whiles, like a puff'd and reckless libertine, himself the primrose path of dalliance treads.

— 第 2 頁 —

波隆尼

還在這兒，雷歐提斯！上船去，上船去！……再聽幾句教訓，記在心裡：不要想到什麼就說什麼。對人要和氣，可是不要過分狎昵。

Yet here, Laertes! aboard, aboard, for shame!... And these few precepts in thy memory... Give thy thoughts no tongue... Be thou familiar, but by no means vulgar.

波隆尼

不要向人告貸，也不要借錢給人——借出去，往往錢和朋友一起丟。

Neither a borrower nor a lender be; for loan oft loses both itself and friend.

不要向人告貸，也不要借錢給人

Neither a borrower nor a lender be.

— 第 3 頁 —

波隆尼

尤其要緊的——你必須對自己忠實。正像有了白晝才有黑夜一樣，對自己忠實，才不會對別人欺詐。

This above all: to thine own self be true, and it must follow, as the night the day, thou canst not then be false to any man.

你必須對自己忠實

To thine own self be true.

雷歐提斯

再會，奧菲莉亞。記住我對你說的話。

Farewell, Ophelia; and remember well what I have said to you.

奧菲莉亞

已經鎖在我的記憶裡了——鑰匙就交給你保管。

'Tis in my memory lock'd, and you yourself shall keep the key of it.

— 第 4 頁 —

奧菲莉亞

父親，殿下最近……的確向我表示過許多情意。

He hath, my lord, of late made many tenders of his affection to me.

波隆尼

情意！哼！傻孩子，那些都是捕捉愚蠢山鷸的圈套。從現在起，不許你再跟哈姆雷特殿下說一句話！

Affection! pooh! you speak like a green girl... springes to catch woodcocks... from this time forth... give words or talk with the Lord Hamlet.

奧菲莉亞

我一定聽您的話，父親。

I shall obey, my lord.

旁白

一句順從。日後，要用一生來償還。

One word of obedience — to be repaid, in time, with a life.
