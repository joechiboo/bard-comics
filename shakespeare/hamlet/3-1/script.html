— 第 1 頁 —

波隆尼

拿著這本書讀，掩飾一下。他來了——我們躲起來吧。

Read on this book; that show of such an exercise may colour your loneliness... I hear him coming: let's withdraw, my lord.

克勞地

（內心）唉，這句話鞭在我的良心上，多重的一鞭！

(aside) How smart a lash that speech doth give my conscience!

哈姆雷特

生存還是毀滅——這是一個值得考慮的問題。

To be, or not to be: that is the question.

生存還是毀滅，這是一個值得考慮的問題

To be, or not to be: that is the question.

— 第 2 頁 —

哈姆雷特

默然忍受命運暴虐的毒箭，或是挺身反抗人世無涯的苦難，哪一種更高貴？死了——睡著了——什麼都完了。

Whether 'tis nobler in the mind to suffer the slings and arrows of outrageous fortune, or to take arms against a sea of troubles, and by opposing end them? To die: to sleep; no more.

哈姆雷特

誰願意負著重擔呻吟流汗——倘不是因為懼怕死後的那個神秘之國，那個從來不曾有一個旅人回來過的地方？

Who would fardels bear, to grunt and sweat under a weary life, but that the dread of something after death, the undiscover'd country from whose bourn no traveller returns, puzzles the will?

從來不曾有一個旅人回來過的神秘之國

The undiscover'd country from whose bourn no traveller returns.

— 第 3 頁 —

奧菲莉亞

殿下，這些是您送我的紀念品——請您收回去吧。

My lord, I have remembrances of yours, that I have longed long to re-deliver; I pray you, now receive them.

哈姆雷特

不，我從來沒有給過你什麼。

No, not I; I never gave you aught.

哈姆雷特

你貞潔嗎？你美麗嗎？……到修道院去吧！為什麼要生一群罪人出來？我們都是十足的壞人——一個也不要相信。去吧！

Ha, ha! are you honest?... Get thee to a nunnery: why wouldst thou be a breeder of sinners?... We are arrant knaves, all; believe none of us. Go thy ways to a nunnery.

到修道院去吧

Get thee to a nunnery.

— 第 4 頁 —

奧菲莉亞

啊，一顆多麼高貴的心，就這樣隕落了！朝臣的眼睛、學者的辯舌、軍人的利劍——國家所矚望的一朵嬌花——全完了。

O, what a noble mind is here o'erthrown! The courtier's, soldier's, scholar's, eye, tongue, sword; the expectancy and rose of the fair state... quite, quite down!

一顆多麼高貴的心，就這樣隕落了

O, what a noble mind is here o'erthrown!

克勞地

戀愛？他的心事不在戀愛上。他說的話雖然顛三倒四，也不像瘋狂。——把他送到英格蘭去。

Love! his affections do not that way tend; nor what he spake, though it lack'd form a little, was not like madness... he shall with speed to England.

— 第 5 頁 —

克勞地

他的靈魂裡孵育著什麼東西……偉大人物的瘋狂，是不能聽其自然的。

There's something in his soul, o'er which his melancholy sits on brood... Madness in great ones must not unwatch'd go.

偉大人物的瘋狂，是不能聽其自然的

Madness in great ones must not unwatch'd go.

旁白

沒有人問過誘餌痛不痛。

No one ever asks the bait whether it bleeds.
