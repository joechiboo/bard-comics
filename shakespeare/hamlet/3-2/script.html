— 第 1 頁 —

哈姆雷特

唸台詞要唸得順口自然。要是像有些伶人那樣張牙舞爪，我寧願叫街上的宣令員來唸——一切不要過火。

Speak the speech, I pray you, as I pronounced it to you, trippingly on the tongue: but if you mouth it... I had as lief the town-crier spoke my lines... o'erstep not the modesty of nature.

哈姆雷特

今晚有一場戲，其中一段情節很像我父親的死。好霍拉旭——替我盯緊我的叔父。

There is a play to-night before the king; one scene of it comes near the circumstance which I have told thee of my father's death... Observe mine uncle... Give him heedful note.

— 第 2 頁 —

克勞地

我們的姪兒過得好嗎？

How fares our cousin Hamlet?

哈姆雷特

好極了——跟變色龍一樣吃空氣過活，塞滿了諾言。

Excellent, i' faith; of the chameleon's dish: I eat the air, promise-crammed.

旁白

啞劇開場——一個人，往睡著的國王耳中，倒進毒藥。

The dumb-show enters: a man pours poison into the sleeping king's ear.

— 第 3 頁 —

伶后

要是我死了丈夫再作新人，讓詛咒永遠跟隨我！

In second husband let me be accurst! None wed the second but who kill'd the first.

哈姆雷特

母親，您覺得這齣戲怎樣？

Madam, how like you this play?

葛簇特

我想那位夫人發的誓，太重了些。

The lady doth protest too much, methinks.

那位夫人發的誓太重了

The lady doth protest too much, methinks.

— 第 4 頁 —

哈姆雷特

他在花園裡毒死了他，篡奪了他的位置。這故事是真事改編——瞧著吧，兇手馬上就要得到王后的愛了。

He poisons him i' the garden for's estate... the story is extant, and writ in choice Italian: you shall see anon how the murderer gets the love of Gonzago's wife.

廷臣

王上站起來了！

The king rises!

克勞地

給我點起火把來！走！

Give me some light: away!

給我點起火把來！走！

Give me some light: away!

— 第 5 頁 —

哈姆雷特

好霍拉旭！那鬼魂的話，一個字也不假——一千鎊我也敢賭。你看見了嗎？

O good Horatio, I'll take the ghost's word for a thousand pound. Didst perceive?

霍拉旭

看得清清楚楚，殿下。

Very well, my lord... I did very well note him.

— 第 6 頁 —

哈姆雷特

你願意吹吹這管笛子嗎？

Will you play upon this pipe?

吉爾登斯坦

殿下，我不會。

My lord, I cannot.

哈姆雷特

你們把我當作什麼樂器？隨便你們把我叫作什麼樂器——你們可以撥弄我，卻不能玩弄我。

Why, look you now, how unworthy a thing you make of me! You would play upon me... call me what instrument you will, though you can fret me, yet you cannot play upon me.

你們可以撥弄我，卻不能玩弄我

Though you can fret me, yet you cannot play upon me.

— 第 7 頁 —

哈姆雷特

現在是深夜的巫魅時刻，墳墓洞開，地獄向人間吐出瘟疫。……我要去見我的母親了。心啊，不要失去你的天性——我要用利劍一樣的說話，可是決不用劍。

Tis now the very witching time of night, when churchyards yawn and hell itself breathes out contagion to this world... Soft! now to my mother... I will speak daggers to her, but use none.

用利劍一樣的說話，可是決不用劍

I will speak daggers to her, but use none.
