3-4 寢宮驚變Bilingual Script

❦

中文為朱生豪公版譯本之白話改寫；英文為莎士比亞公版原文。

— 第 1 頁 —

波隆尼

他來了。請您狠狠教訓他——我就躲在這裡。

He will come straight. Look you lay home to him... I'll sconce me even here. Pray you, be round with him.

葛簇特

哈姆雷特，你把你的父親大大得罪了。

Hamlet, thou hast thy father much offended.

哈姆雷特

母親，你把我的父親大大得罪了。

Mother, you have my father much offended.

— 第 2 頁 —

葛簇特

你要幹什麼？你不是要殺我吧？——救命！救命啊！

What wilt thou do? thou wilt not murder me? Help, help, ho!

波隆尼

（帷幕後）救命！

(behind) What, ho! help, help, help!

哈姆雷特

怎麼！一隻耗子？死吧！

How now! a rat? Dead, for a ducat, dead!

一隻耗子？死吧！

How now! a rat? Dead, for a ducat, dead!

— 第 3 頁 —

葛簇特

啊，多麼鹵莽殘酷的行為！

O me, what hast thou done?... O, what a rash and bloody deed is this!

哈姆雷特

殘酷的行為？好媽媽——跟殺了一個國王、再嫁給他的兄弟，差不多同樣壞。

A bloody deed! almost as bad, good mother, as kill a king, and marry with his brother.

哈姆雷特

你這倒楣的、鹵莽的、愛管閒事的傻瓜，再會！我還以為是一個在你上面的人。

Thou wretched, rash, intruding fool, farewell! I took thee for thy better.

— 第 4 頁 —

哈姆雷特

瞧這一幅畫像，再瞧這一幅。這是你的前夫，額角上帶著天神的威嚴；這是你的現任丈夫——像一株霉爛的禾穗。你有眼睛嗎？

Look here, upon this picture, and on this... See, what a grace was seated on this brow... Here is your husband; like a mildew'd ear... Have you eyes?

你有眼睛嗎？

Have you eyes?

葛簇特

啊，哈姆雷特，別說下去了！你使我的眼睛看進了我自己的靈魂——那上面的黑點，洗都洗不掉。

O Hamlet, speak no more: thou turn'st mine eyes into my very soul; and there I see such black and grained spots as will not leave their tinct.

— 第 5 頁 —

哈姆雷特

天使保佑我！……您是來責備您兒子的拖延嗎？

Save me, and hover o'er me with your wings, you heavenly guards!... Do you not come your tardy son to chide?

鬼魂

別忘記。我來，是要磨快你快要鈍了的決心。可是瞧——你的母親多麼驚恐，替她分憂吧。

Do not forget: this visitation is but to whet thy almost blunted purpose. But, look, amazement on thy mother sits... speak to her, Hamlet.

葛簇特

唉，他瘋了——對著空氣說話。你在跟誰說話？我什麼也沒看見。

Alas, he's mad!... To whom do you speak this?... Nothing at all; yet all that is I see.

— 第 6 頁 —

哈姆雷特

母親——向上天懺悔過去，警戒未來。不要上我叔父的床。今晚忍一忍，下一次就容易一些。

Confess yourself to heaven; repent what's past; avoid what is to come... Go not to mine uncle's bed... Refrain to-night, and that shall lend a kind of easiness to the next abstinence.

葛簇特

啊，哈姆雷特，你把我的心劈成兩半了。

O Hamlet, thou hast cleft my heart in twain.

哈姆雷特

那就把壞的一半丟掉，用另外一半好好過活吧。

O, throw away the worser part of it, and live the purer with the other half.

你把我的心劈成兩半了

Thou hast cleft my heart in twain.

— 第 7 頁 —

哈姆雷特

我必須到英格蘭去，你知道嗎？……這位大臣生前是個嘮叨的傻瓜，現在倒是嚴肅沉默得很。——晚安，母親。

I must to England; you know that?... This counsellor is now most still, most secret and most grave, who was in life a foolish prating knave. — Good night, mother.

晚安，母親

Good night, mother.
