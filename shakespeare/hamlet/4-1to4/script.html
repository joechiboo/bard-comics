— 第 1 頁 —

葛簇特

他瘋得像互相爭勝的大海和狂風。他聽見帷幕後有動靜，拔劍就刺——把那位好老人家殺了。

Mad as the sea and wind, when both contend which is the mightier: in his lawless fit, behind the arras hearing something stir, whips out his rapier... and... kills the unseen good old man.

克勞地

要是我在那裡，躺下的就是我。放著他這樣，對誰都是威脅——這件事必須小心處置。

It had been so with us, had we been there: his liberty is full of threats to all... this vile deed we must, with all our majesty and skill, both countenance and excuse.

— 第 2 頁 —

羅森克蘭茲

殿下，屍體在哪裡？

What have you done, my lord, with the dead body?

哈姆雷特

屍體和國王在一起，可是國王沒有和屍體在一起。國王是一件東西——

The body is with the king, but the king is not with the body. The king is a thing—

克勞地

哈姆雷特，波隆尼在哪裡？

Now, Hamlet, where's Polonius?

哈姆雷特

在吃晚飯。不是他在吃，是蛆蟲在吃他——一場政治性的蟲宴。

At supper... Not where he eats, but where he is eaten: a certain convocation of politic worms are e'en at him.

— 第 3 頁 —

克勞地

你必須立刻去英格蘭。

Therefore prepare thyself... every thing is bent for England.

哈姆雷特

好——去英格蘭！再會，親愛的母親。

For England! Good. — Farewell, dear mother.

克勞地

是你慈愛的父親，哈姆雷特。

Thy loving father, Hamlet.

哈姆雷特

父親跟母親是夫妻，夫妻是一體。所以——再會，我的母親。

My mother: father and mother is man and wife; man and wife is one flesh; and so, my mother.

克勞地

英格蘭王啊——照我密函上的指示辦：把哈姆雷特立刻處死。辦好它，英格蘭。

And, England... thou mayst not coldly set our sovereign process; which imports at full... the present death of Hamlet. Do it, England.

把哈姆雷特立刻處死。辦好它，英格蘭

The present death of Hamlet. Do it, England.

— 第 4 頁 —

哈姆雷特

兩萬個人，為了一塊還不夠埋葬他們的土地，像上床一樣走向墳墓。而我呢——父親被害、母親受辱，理由比天大，卻讓一切沉睡。

The imminent death of twenty thousand men, that, for a fantasy and trick of fame, go to their graves like beds, fight for a plot... which is not tomb enough... How stand I then, that have a father kill'd, a mother stain'd... and let all sleep?

哈姆雷特

啊，從這一刻起——讓我的思想只染血色，不然就一文不值！

O, from this time forth, my thoughts be bloody, or be nothing worth!

讓我的思想只染血色，不然就一文不值

My thoughts be bloody, or be nothing worth!

— 第 5 頁 —

旁白

船向英格蘭去。船上載著兩個同學、一封要他項上人頭的信——和一個已經下定決心的王子。

The ship sails for England, carrying two schoolfellows, a letter that asks for his head — and a prince whose mind is made up at last.
