— 第 1 頁 —

奧菲莉亞

（唱）他死了，不見了，姑娘，他死了，不見了；他頭上是青青的草，腳下是一塊石頭。

(sings) He is dead and gone, lady, he is dead and gone; at his head a grass-green turf, at his heels a stone.

克勞地

悲哀來的時候，不是單個來的，是成群結隊來的——她的父親被殺、你的兒子遠放、人民議論紛紛，現在又是這個可憐的孩子。

O Gertrude, Gertrude, when sorrows come, they come not single spies, but in battalions... poor Ophelia divided from herself and her fair judgment.

悲哀是成群結隊來的

When sorrows come, they come not single spies, but in battalions.

— 第 2 頁 —

雷歐提斯

你這萬惡的國王！把我的父親還給我！

O thou vile king, give me my father!

克勞地

冷靜點，好雷歐提斯。國王有神明護衛，叛逆只能在一邊乾瞪眼。告訴我你的父親怎麼死的——罪不在我。

What is the cause, Laertes, that thy rebellion looks so giant-like?... There's such divinity doth hedge a king, that treason can but peep to what it would... I am guiltless of your father's death.

— 第 3 頁 —

奧菲莉亞

這是迷迭香，是代表回憶的——親愛的，請你記著。這是三色堇，是代表想念的。

There's rosemary, that's for remembrance; pray, love, remember: and there is pansies, that's for thoughts.

迷迭香，代表回憶

Rosemary, that's for remembrance.

雷歐提斯

憂愁、苦難、悲哀和地獄的磨折——在她身上，都變成了可憐可愛。

Thought and affliction, passion, hell itself, she turns to favour and to prettiness.

— 第 4 頁 —

克勞地

殺你父親的人回來了。我們安排一場友誼比劍——他生性寬厚，不會檢查劍刃。你只要挑一柄沒有包頭的利劍……

Hamlet comes back... We'll put on those shall praise your excellence... He, being remiss, most generous and free from all contriving, will not peruse the foils; so that, with ease... you may choose a sword unbated.

雷歐提斯

我還有一服毒藥，劍尖只要沾上一點，見血就是死。

I bought an unction of a mountebank, so mortal that, but dip a knife in it, where it draws blood no cataplasm so rare... can save the thing from death that is but scratch'd withal.

克勞地

再備一杯毒酒。萬一他躲過你的劍——那杯酒不會失手。

I ha't... A chalice for the nonce, whereon but sipping, if he by chance escape your venom'd stuck, our purpose may hold there.

— 第 5 頁 —

葛簇特

一株柳樹橫在溪水上。她編的花圈掉進水裡，她也跟著落下去——衣裳張開，把她像人魚一樣托在水面，她嘴裡還斷斷續續唱著古老的歌。

There is a willow grows aslant a brook... an envious sliver broke; when down her weedy trophies and herself fell in the weeping brook. Her clothes spread wide; and, mermaid-like, awhile they bore her up: which time she chanted snatches of old tunes.

一株柳樹橫在溪水上

There is a willow grows aslant a brook.

雷歐提斯

可憐的奧菲莉亞，你的水已經太多了，我禁住我的眼淚。

Too much of water hast thou, poor Ophelia, and therefore I forbid my tears.

— 第 6 頁 —

旁白

歌聲沉了下去。復仇的計畫，也定了下來。

The song sinks below the water; and the plot is sealed.
