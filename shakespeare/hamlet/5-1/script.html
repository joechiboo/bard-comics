— 第 1 頁 —

掘墓人

（唱）一把鋤頭一把鍬，一件屍衣把身包；挖一個土坑深又深，來了個客人剛剛好。

(sings) A pick-axe, and a spade, a spade, for and a shrouding sheet: O, a pit of clay for to be made for such a guest is meet.

哈姆雷特

這傢伙一邊掘墳一邊唱歌——他不覺得這工作悲哀嗎？

Has this fellow no feeling of his business, that he sings at grave-making?

霍拉旭

做慣了，也就不在乎了。

Custom hath made it in him a property of easiness.

— 第 2 頁 —

哈姆雷特

這個骷髏也許是個律師。他的狡辯、他的訟案、他的產業條款——現在都到哪裡去了？

Why may not that be the skull of a lawyer? Where be his quiddities now, his quillets, his cases, his tenures, and his tricks?

掘墓人

這一個啊，先生——是郁利克的骷髏，先王的弄人。在土裡躺了二十三年了。

This same skull, sir, was Yorick's skull, the king's jester... Here's a skull now; this skull has lain in the earth three and twenty years.

— 第 3 頁 —

哈姆雷特

唉，可憐的郁利克！霍拉旭，我認識他——他有取之不竭的詼諧，背過我一千次。你的笑話呢？你的歌呢？現在，你還會嘲笑你自己的獰笑嗎？

Alas, poor Yorick! I knew him, Horatio: a fellow of infinite jest, of most excellent fancy: he hath borne me on his back a thousand times... Where be your gibes now? your gambols? your songs?... Not one now, to mock your own grinning?

唉，可憐的郁利克！

Alas, poor Yorick!

哈姆雷特

凱撒死了，變成泥土——也許哪一天，會拿去堵一個酒桶的口。

Imperious Caesar, dead and turn'd to clay, might stop a hole to keep the wind away.

— 第 4 頁 —

哈姆雷特

是誰的葬禮？儀式這樣簡略——是自己了斷性命的人嗎？瞧，雷歐提斯也在。

Who is this they follow? And with such maimed rites? This doth betoken the corse they follow did with desperate hand fordo its own life... That is Laertes, a very noble youth: mark.

葛簇特

好花是應當散在美人身上的。永別了——我本來希望你做我哈姆雷特的妻子。

Sweets to the sweet: farewell! I hoped thou shouldst have been my Hamlet's wife.

好花是應當散在美人身上的

Sweets to the sweet: farewell!

— 第 5 頁 —

雷歐提斯

等一等——讓我再抱她一次！現在，把泥土倒在生者和死者身上吧！

Hold off the earth awhile, till I have caught her once more in mine arms: now pile your dust upon the quick and dead.

哈姆雷特

誰的悲哀能有這樣誇張？——我，丹麥王子哈姆雷特在此！

What is he whose grief bears such an emphasis?... This is I, Hamlet the Dane.

丹麥王子哈姆雷特在此

This is I, Hamlet the Dane.

— 第 6 頁 —

哈姆雷特

我愛奧菲莉亞——四萬個兄弟的愛加起來，也抵不過我對她的愛！

I loved Ophelia: forty thousand brothers could not, with all their quantity of love, make up my sum.

四萬個兄弟的愛，也抵不過我

Forty thousand brothers could not... make up my sum.

克勞地

忍耐點，好雷歐提斯——想想我們昨天晚上商量的事。這件事很快就有一個了結。

Good Laertes... Strengthen your patience in our last night's speech; we'll put the matter to the present push.
