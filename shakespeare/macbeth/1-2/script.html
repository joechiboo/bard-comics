— 第 1 頁 —

旁白

福累斯附近的軍營。戰火剛歇。

A camp near Forres. The battle has just ended.

鄧肯

那個渾身是血的人是誰？看他的樣子，該有前線最新的消息。

What bloody man is that? He can report, as seemeth by his plight, of the revolt the newest state.

馬爾康

是他！當初奮戰救我突圍的軍曹。勇敢的朋友，把你離開時的戰況稟告王上！

This is the sergeant who like a good and hardy soldier fought 'gainst my captivity. Hail, brave friend!

— 第 2 頁 —

軍曹

勝負未分——兩軍像兩個力竭的泳者扭抱在一起，誰也使不出本事。

Doubtful it stood, as two spent swimmers that do cling together and choke their art.

軍曹（旁白）

叛徒麥克唐華德——天下的奸惡都聚在他一身，連命運都像娼妓一樣對他賣笑。

The merciless Macdonwald—worthy to be a rebel—and Fortune, on his damned quarrel smiling, showed like a rebel's whore.

— 第 3 頁 —

軍曹（旁白）

可是英勇的馬克白——他當得起這名字——根本不把命運放在眼裡。他揮著染血的劍，像煞星一樣殺開一條路，直取那奴才面前！

For brave Macbeth—well he deserves that name—disdaining Fortune, with his brandished steel, which smoked with bloody execution, like valor's minion carved out his passage till he faced the slave.

軍曹

不行禮，不搭話，一劍從肚臍剖到下巴——那顆頭，已經掛上我們的城樓。

He unseamed him from the nave to th' chops, and fixed his head upon our battlements.

鄧肯

啊，英勇的表弟！尊貴的壯士！

O valiant cousin, worthy gentleman!

— 第 4 頁 —

軍曹

但風暴偏從東方捲來——挪威王看準時機，率一批甲械精良的生力軍，發動新一波猛攻！

The Norweyan lord, surveying vantage, with furbished arms and new supplies of men began a fresh assault.

鄧肯

我們的將軍——馬克白和班柯，沒有因此膽怯吧？

Dismayed not this our captains, Macbeth and Banquo?

軍曹

膽怯？除非麻雀嚇得退老鷹、兔子嚇得走雄獅！他們像裝了雙倍火藥的巨炮，愈打愈猛——咳……我不行了，傷口得治……

Yes, as sparrows eagles, or the hare the lion... as cannons overcharged with double cracks. But I am faint; my gashes cry for help.

鄧肯

你的話和你的傷一樣，都透著戰士的光榮。送他去軍醫！

So well thy words become thee as thy wounds: they smack of honor both. Go get him surgeons.

— 第 5 頁 —

列諾克斯

瞧他眼裡的慌張——像有天大的事要說。

What a haste looks through his eyes! So should he look that seems to speak things strange.

洛斯

上帝保佑吾王！

God save the King.

洛斯

在費輔，挪威王親率大軍，還有叛徒考特爵士相助——但馬克白披甲迎戰，刀來槍往，硬是挫了他的凶焰。勝利是我們的！

Norway himself, with terrible numbers, assisted by that most disloyal traitor the Thane of Cawdor... the victory fell on us.

洛斯

挪威王史威諾求和了。不繳一萬塊錢，就不准他埋葬戰死的將士。

Sweno, the Norways' king, craves composition. Nor would we deign him burial of his men till he disbursed ten thousand dollars.

— 第 6 頁 —

鄧肯

考特爵士再也騙不了我。傳令——立即處死。他的爵位，移贈馬克白。

No more that Thane of Cawdor shall deceive our bosom interest. Go pronounce his present death, and with his former title greet Macbeth.

洛斯

遵命。

I'll see it done.

他所失去的，也就是尊貴的馬克白所得到的

What he hath lost, noble Macbeth hath won.

旁白

一個頭銜換了主人。而荒野上，有三雙眼睛正等著它的新主人……

A title changes hands. And out on the heath, three pairs of eyes await its new owner…
