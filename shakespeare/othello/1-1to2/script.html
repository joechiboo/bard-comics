— 第 1 頁 —

旁白

威尼斯。深夜。有人剛剛帶走了元老勃拉班修的女兒——消息最快的，永遠是心懷鬼胎的人。

Venice, deep in the night. A senator's daughter has just been carried away — and the first to know are those with poison in their hearts.

羅德利哥

你一直花我的錢，卻早知道這件事——伊阿古，你太不夠朋友了！

Tush! never tell me; I take it much unkindly that thou, Iago, who hast had my purse as if the strings were thine, shouldst know of this.

伊阿古

他媽的！你根本不聽我說。我恨那摩爾人——他把副將的位置給了那個只會紙上談兵的佛羅倫斯人卡西歐，而我，只落得替他扛旗。

'Sblood, but you will not hear me... I know my price, I am worth no worse a place... 'Despise me, if I do not.' He, in good time, must his lieutenant be, and I—God bless the mark!—his Moorship's ancient.

— 第 2 頁 —

伊阿古

我跟隨他，其實是跟隨我自己。天知道——我不是為了忠心和義務，只是假裝忠心，等著替自己打算。世人所知道的我，並不是實在的我。

In following him, I follow but myself; heaven is my judge, not I for love and duty, but seeming so, for my peculiar end... I am not what I am.

世人所知道的我，並不是實在的我

I am not what I am.

伊阿古

去，把她父親叫醒！大聲嚷，讓全城都聽見——喂！勃拉班修！勃拉班修老爺！起來！有賊！

Call up her father, rouse him... Awake! what, ho, Brabantio! thieves! thieves! thieves!

— 第 3 頁 —

勃拉班修

半夜三更鬼叫什麼？你們是什麼人？

What is the reason of this terrible summons? What is the matter there?

伊阿古

（黑暗中）老爺，您的心要碎了——就在這會兒，一頭老黑羊正在跟您的白母羊交配！起來，起來！

(from the dark) 'Zounds, sir, you're robb'd... Even now, now, very now, an old black ram is topping your white ewe. Arise, arise!

勃拉班修

點起火把！叫醒我所有的人！——真的走了！黛絲德夢娜！啊，被騙走的父親！

Strike on the tinder, ho! Give me a taper! call up all my people!... It is too true an evil: gone she is.

伊阿古

（低聲）我得走了。讓我這面旗，繼續插在摩爾人身邊。

(aside) Farewell; for I must leave you... I must show out a flag and sign of love.

— 第 4 頁 —

伊阿古

將軍，那老頭兒說話太難聽，我幾乎忍不住要捅他。他們就要來抓您了——躲一躲吧？

Nine or ten times I had thought to have yerk'd him here under the ribs... He will divorce you... You were best go in.

奧賽羅

不必。讓他們來。我的人品、我的地位和我無愧的良心，自然會替我說話。

Not I; I must be found: my parts, my title and my perfect soul shall manifest me rightly.

奧賽羅

把你們雪亮的劍收起來吧——夜露會鏽壞它們的。

Keep up your bright swords, for the dew will rust them.

把你們雪亮的劍收起來吧，夜露會鏽壞它們

Keep up your bright swords, for the dew will rust them.

— 第 5 頁 —

勃拉班修

萬惡的賊！你把我的女兒藏到哪裡去了？你一定是用魔法迷住了她——不然她怎麼會逃出閨房，投進你這黑炭一樣的胸口！

O thou foul thief, where hast thou stow'd my daughter? Damn'd as thou art, thou hast enchanted her... Run from her guardage to the sooty bosom of such a thing as thou.

軍官

兩位大人，公爵正連夜召開緊急會議——土耳其艦隊正撲向賽普勒斯，點名要奧賽羅將軍。

The duke's in council and your noble self, I am sure, is sent for... The Turkish fleet makes for Cyprus.

勃拉班修

好，那就到公爵面前評理去！

Away to council... I'll have't disputed on.

— 第 6 頁 —

旁白

同一個晚上——戰爭與控訴，一起送到了公爵的桌上。

In one and the same night, war and accusation arrive together at the Duke's table.

伊阿古

（自語）錢包裡有錢的傻瓜跟著我，摩爾人信任我。好戲——才剛剛開場。

(aside) Thus do I ever make my fool my purse... The Moor is of a free and open nature... Hell and night must bring this monstrous birth to the world's light.
